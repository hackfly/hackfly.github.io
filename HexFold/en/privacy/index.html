HexFold Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Robotic Jam LLC ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in HexFold.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name or contact information (unless you email us)
  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not collect location data
  • Game progress is stored locally on your device only

Email Feedback

HexFold includes an option to send feedback via email. If you contact us, we will only use your email address to respond to your feedback. We do not add you to mailing lists or share your email with third parties.

Advertising

HexFold displays advertisements through Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect:

  • IP address (used to estimate general location)
  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics
  • App usage data and interactions with ads

This data is collected by Google to serve and measure advertisements. For more information, see How Google Uses Data.

GDPR Consent (European Users): When you first launch HexFold in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you will be presented with a consent dialog. You can choose whether to allow personalized advertising or see non-personalized ads only. You can change your choice at any time through the Privacy Settings option in the app.

Opting Out of Personalized Ads:

  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads, and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

Removing Ads: You may purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app option to disable all advertising. No advertising data is collected when ads are removed.

Third-Party Services

HexFold uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google processes data according to its own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Privacy
  • How Google Uses Data from Partner Apps

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment information.

Your Privacy Rights

European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Right to Access: Request information about data collected about you
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your data for advertising purposes

You can manage your advertising consent through the Privacy Settings option in HexFold. For other requests, contact us at the address below.

Your Privacy Choices (California Users)

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Opt out of cross-context behavioral advertising

We do not sell personal information. When personalized ads are enabled, we may "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined under the CPRA. To opt out, use the Privacy Settings option in HexFold (which controls personalized advertising), or follow the device-level controls described above. To exercise other rights, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

HexFold is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Security

All data transmitted to advertising partners is encrypted using TLS. We do not store personal data on our own servers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date.
