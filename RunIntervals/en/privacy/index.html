RunIntervals Privacy Policy

Robotic Jam LLC · Last updated: November 18, 2026

RunIntervals (iPhone and Apple Watch) does not collect, store, or track any information about you. It has no ads, no analytics, and no accounts.

Apple Health

Health features are off unless you turn on “Use RunIntervals to record your workout” in the app’s Settings. If you turn it on:

  • The app saves each run to Apple Health as a workout.
  • During a run, it reads that workout’s distance, heart rate, and calories from Apple Health, to show them on screen, to tell you when you reach a distance goal, and to show a summary when you stop.
  • It reads your recent workouts so that a run is saved only once when another app is also recording.

This information stays in Apple Health on your own devices. It is never sent to Robotic Jam or to anyone else, and it is never used for advertising or marketing. You can turn the setting off at any time, and you can change or remove the app’s access in the Health app.

Other things worth knowing

  • Your settings (intervals, alert type, goal) are kept on your device and synced directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Siri and Shortcuts requests are handled by Apple; the app only receives the action to perform.
  • Goal notifications, if you turn them on, are created on your device.
  • Tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. I never see your payment details.

Feedback

There is a link in the app to send feedback by email. If you use it, I will only use your email address to respond to your feedback.
